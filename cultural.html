📌 Important Guidelines
  • Registration is required for participation in the cultural events.
  • Participants should complete registration before the announced registration deadline.
  • Participants should report to the venue at least 15 minutes before the scheduled event time.
  • Participants are requested to follow the event timings and instructions provided by the organizing committee.
  • Participation is subject to available slots, event requirements and organizer confirmation where applicable.
  • Parents/guardians are responsible for children participating in the events.
  • Participants should maintain discipline and respect the venue and other participants.
  • Any changes to event timing, venue or registration status will be communicated through the SSF Ganesha Mahotsav portal and official communication channels.
